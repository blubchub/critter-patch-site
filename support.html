Critter Patch Support

Questions, a bug, or an idea? Open an issue (a free GitHub account is needed) and you'll get an answer there.

How to play

  • Tap a critter to highlight its group (two or more matching critters that touch), then tap it again, press the "+N ✓" button at the top left, or use Double Tap to clear it.
  • Turn the Digital Crown to move the highlight from group to group.
  • Bigger groups score far more. Clear the whole board for a Tidy Patch bonus.

Questions

Where is my progress saved? Only on your Apple Watch. It isn't sent anywhere, so deleting the app deletes your progress.

The critters are hard to see or tap. Turn on Big Critters in Settings for a roomier 5 × 5 board, and Color-blind patterns for a mark on each kind of critter. Quick Clear clears a group with one tap. VoiceOver, Reduce Motion, and Larger Text are supported.

How does Sprout Rush work? After you press Start, new rows grow in on a clock that keeps speeding up. Clear groups to keep any column from overflowing. The clock pauses when you open the menu or lower your wrist.

Is there sound? Yes, off by default: turn on Sound in Settings.
