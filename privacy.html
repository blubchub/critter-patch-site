Critter Patch Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 4, 2026

Critter Patch does not collect any data.

  • The game works fully offline. Your progress, settings, petals, and garden are saved only on your Apple Watch.
  • There are no ads, no analytics, no tracking, no accounts, and no in-app purchases.
  • Nothing you do in the game is sent to us or to anyone else.
  • Game Center (only if a future version turns on leaderboards and you are signed in to Game Center): your Daily Seed score, your Sprout Rush score and time, and your Garden Path star total would be sent to Apple's Game Center under your Game Center name. Apple's privacy policy covers Game Center.

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at the same address and shown in the app.
